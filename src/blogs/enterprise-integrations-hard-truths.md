In the aftermath of a failed integration post-mortem, it became clear that APIs are rarely the true blockers. For CIOs and delivery leaders, the real risks are hidden in ownership gaps, shifting timelines, and unspoken assumptions.

Teams consistently underestimate the coordination cost. External dependencies move at their own pace, priorities shift, and documentation is always a step behind reality. The most successful integrations are those with explicit ownership, regular checkpoints, and robust contingency plans.

Ignoring these hard truths turns integrations into silent schedule killers. As your platform ambitions grow, will your integration strategy keep pace—or will unseen risks quietly erode your delivery confidence?
